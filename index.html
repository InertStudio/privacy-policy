Privacy Policy

Publisher: InertStudio

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how mobile games published by InertStudio handle information, including when they display advertisements through Google AdMob.

1. Scope

This policy applies to Android mobile games published by InertStudio that link to this page from their Google Play listing or from within the app (together, “our games”). It is intended for games that use Google AdMob banner and/or rewarded advertisements. If a game uses additional data-collecting services, we will update this policy or provide an additional game-specific notice.

2. Information handled through our games

Our games may store game progress, scores, settings, and preferences locally on your device so that gameplay can continue and preferences can be remembered. Local data is generally not sent to us unless a particular game explicitly says otherwise.

Our games use Google AdMob to show advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information with Google, depending on the device, user settings, applicable law, and SDK configuration, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • Device and app identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID, where available and permitted;
  • App and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, and video views;
  • Diagnostic and performance data, such as app launch time, stability indicators, and technical performance information.

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the information you choose to include in your message.

3. How information is used

Information may be processed to display and measure advertisements (including personalized or non-personalized ads, as applicable), deliver optional rewarded-video experiences, prevent advertising fraud and abuse, maintain the stability and security of ad services, save local gameplay preferences, and respond to support inquiries.

4. Advertising and third-party services

Our games may display banner ads and rewarded ads powered by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Rewarded ads are optional advertisements that users choose to view in exchange for an in-game benefit where available. Google may process information in accordance with its own policies and advertising settings.

For more information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • Google My Ad Center

Depending on the advertising configuration, Google may use advertising partners. Their processing is governed by applicable notices and consent choices.

5. Consent and advertising choices

Where required by applicable law, advertising-related data processing is subject to appropriate disclosures and consent choices. This may include a consent message within a game for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or other relevant locations. Where an in-app privacy options control is available, it can be used to review or change those choices.

You may also manage some advertising preferences and identifiers through your Android device settings and Google My Ad Center. Changing ad preferences does not necessarily remove all advertisements.

6. Information sharing

Advertising-related information is transmitted to and processed by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad delivery, measurement, reporting, and fraud prevention. We may receive aggregated advertising reports. Information sent to Google may be processed on infrastructure located in countries other than your own, subject to Google's applicable safeguards and policies. We do not publish your locally stored game progress to third parties under this policy.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game progress and preferences saved locally generally remain on your device until you reset the game, clear its app data, or uninstall it, subject to your device's backup and restore settings. Emails sent to us are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to address the inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations.

Google manages the retention of information processed through AdMob according to its own policies. You can use Google's privacy controls and contact us at the address below with questions or requests relating to information handled by us.

8. Security

We aim to limit data handling to what is needed for gameplay and advertising. Google states that the Google Mobile Ads SDK encrypts the data it transmits using transport-layer security. No method of data storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

Games intended for children, or games made available to a mixed audience that includes children, may require additional disclosures, age-appropriate advertising settings, and other protections. Such requirements are evaluated for each game, including Google Play's Families policies and applicable children's privacy laws. If you have a concern about a child's information, please contact us.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data that we hold, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent where applicable. Contact us using the details below to exercise rights in relation to data handled by us. For information processed independently by Google, consult Google's privacy controls and policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our games, advertising services, or legal obligations change. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date.
